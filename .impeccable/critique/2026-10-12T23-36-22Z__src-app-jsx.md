## Design Specificity
Theme is authored (Hextech navy/gold, Cinzel, client-like bevels). Structure is generic stats-dashboard + filterable card grid: no alphabet/sequence, "Up next" is the 6th equal tile, done/current/upcoming look the same, share card has no champions. Detector: 1 source finding (gradient-text h1, index.css:282); browser: undersized label text (5 label styles <11px ×173 cards), 3 real low-contrast failures (placeholder 3.5:1, .champ-index 2.7:1, footer 2.5:1), dark-glow ×4–5, nested stat cards, About line length ~120ch, flat type scale (1.22 max step); MOBILE text-overflow ×304: .champ-name collapses to ~22px, titles wrap one word per line.
